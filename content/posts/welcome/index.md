# Welcome!

My name is **Dominic** and as of writing this, I am 26 years old.

I'm not 100% sure what kind of content I will be posting on this blog, but it's most likely going to fall within some of the things listed below:
- **Anime** 🗾
- **Life Updates** 🐚
- **Music** 🎵
- **Photography** *(Film & Digital)* 📷
- **Self-Hosting / Homelabbing** 🖥️
- **TV/Movies** 🎥
- **Video Games** 🎮

I want to admit that I'm not entirely making this for an audience although I feel that it's kind of the point of creating a blog that is publicly accessible. I first and foremost want to make this for myself and create/write about things that I find interesting and worth talking about, and hope that others feel the same and end up finding their way here at some point.

With all of that said, I want to welcome all of said people who happened to find their way here! I hope you find some sort of value in my blog :)
